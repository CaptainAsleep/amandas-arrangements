The Designer

An eye for the season, a hand for the detail

Amanda built her practice on a simple standard: a home should always look as though the season just arrived on its own. Every wreath, branch, and arrangement is chosen and placed by hand, then archived with the same care until it's needed again.

She works exclusively in-home, by appointment, with a small roster of clients — so every installation gets a designer's full attention, not a crew's.
